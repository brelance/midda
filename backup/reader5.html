In this world filled with Showa retro vibes, the text also carries a unique texture.

在这个充满昭和复古气息的世界里，文字也带着一种独特的质感。

Every line, every paragraph, seems to be typed from an old-fashioned typewriter, carrying the scent of ink and the mark of the era.

每一行，每一段，都像是从老式打字机中敲出，带着油墨的香气和时代的印记。

The brutalist borders and heavy shadows firmly fix this reading space on the screen, as if a physical book is spread open before your eyes.

粗野主义的边框和厚重的阴影，将这片阅读空间牢牢地固定在屏幕上，仿佛一本实体书被摊开在眼前。

Its design language is direct and honest, without any unnecessary decoration, serving only the content.

它的设计语言直接而坦诚，没有任何不必要的装饰，只为内容服务。

New Discoveries

As the protagonist embarks on his journey, he discovers that this world is not as simple as he imagined.

当主人公踏上旅途，他发现这个世界并非他想象中那样简单。

Secrets are hidden in the depths of the olive-green forests, and unknown giant birds fly under the pale blue sky.

橄榄绿的森林深处隐藏着秘密，浅灰蓝的天空下飞过不知名的巨鸟。
